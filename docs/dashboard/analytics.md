# Analytics

Quantlete provides training analysis tools to track your fitness and performance.

See [Calculations](../calculations.md) for formulas and data sources.

## Training Load

The training load page (`/training-load`) tracks:

### Fitness (CTL)

Chronic Training Load - your long-term training stress, calculated from a 42-day rolling average of training stress score (TSS).

### Fatigue (ATL)

Acute Training Load - your short-term fatigue, calculated from a 7-day rolling average of TSS.

### Form (TSB)

Training Stress Balance - the difference between Fitness and Fatigue. Indicates readiness:
- **Positive TSB** - Fresh, ready for hard efforts
- **Negative TSB** - Fatigued, need recovery
- **Near zero** - Balanced training

### Charts

- Historical fitness/fatigue trends
- Form over time
- Training load distribution

## Power Analysis

The power page (`/power`) shows cycling power metrics:

### Power Curve

Your best power outputs at different durations:
- 5 seconds (sprint)
- 1 minute
- 5 minutes
- 20 minutes (FTP estimate)
- 1 hour

### Power Zones

Distribution of time in power zones:
- Zone 1: Active Recovery
- Zone 2: Endurance
- Zone 3: Tempo
- Zone 4: Threshold
- Zone 5: VO2max
- Zone 6: Anaerobic
- Zone 7: Neuromuscular

### FTP Tracking

Your Functional Threshold Power over time. Set manually or estimate from 20-minute power.

## Eddington Number

The Eddington page (`/eddington`) tracks your cycling Eddington number:

- **E number** - The largest number E where you've cycled E kilometers (or miles) on E or more days
- Progress toward next number
- Historical trend
- Days contributing to current number

## Monthly Statistics

The monthly stats page (`/monthly-stats`) shows:

- Monthly totals (distance, time, elevation)
- Year-over-year comparisons
- Activity counts by type
- Average metrics per activity

## Year in Review

The rewind page (`/rewind`) provides Strava Rewind-style summaries:

- Yearly totals
- Top activities
- Biggest week/month
- Achievement highlights
- Trends compared to previous year
